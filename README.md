# Brass & Aether

The Windows playtest includes Easy, Medium, and Hard expeditions in Brass Frontier, Glass Foundry, Crown Relay, and Moonwreck Docks, plus a separate practice arena. Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and press F5.

## Playtest

From the title screen, choose **New Expedition**, select **Brass Frontier**, and start on **Easy** to draft a hero, relic, and starting tile. You can enter an optional seed on the difficulty screen. Win the opening fight, pick rewards, then travel a seeded route through battles, elites, shops, events, and a rest site. Health, relics, gold, and tile layouts carry between stops. The game saves between fights and after loadout changes. **Continue** picks up from the saved checkpoint.

Each class has 18 Commons and eight Uncommons in its combat reward deck. Use **View class deck** on a hero or recruit card to inspect each tile and the class's signature Uncommon. After combat, choose or skip a tile for each hero in party order. Tiles go to that hero's timeline when space allows, then to shared storage. The expedition saves after each pick. Recruits enter with one starter tile after the current party takes its boss rewards.

Enemies fill a charge gauge above their heads before an attack. Tiles show damage and fire on their right edge. Each hero's timeline shows a portrait, live health, and targeting buttons. The left and right arrows select nearest and farthest. The solid and hollow hearts select highest and lowest HP. The **All** buttons apply the same rule to compatible tiles on the lane.

Open **Battle review** after a fight to inspect activations, misses, relic bonuses, and boss counters. Selecting an activation highlights the tile and its firing beat. Expedition defeats open the review automatically. **Retry in Practice** replays the original encounter with an editable copy of your equipment and pre-fight health.

Defeat the Tollkeeper on Easy to bank Aether Marks and unlock **Medium**. Open the **Aether Chart** from the title or after a run to inspect prerequisites and spend Marks on permanent upgrades between expeditions.

In the **Artifice** branch, unlock **Gravity Hook**, then **Blink Artifice** to forge the Blink family and add its upgrades to future rewards. Three touching copies of the same tile open both choices at the next rarity. A camp can advance one eligible tile instead of healing the party. **Wayfarer's Pin** adds a starter relic choice that grants each hero 6 Guard on their first successful teleport each loop. It can also appear in shops and elite rewards.

**Marksman's Arsenal** unlocks precision fire and shots that hit clustered enemies. Precision tiles let heroes keep their distance when the lane has no melee attacks. **Bastion Workshop** unlocks counters fueled by Guard and tiles that shield vulnerable allies. Shops offer one specialist upgrade from the unlocked families, from Uncommon on the first leg to Rare on the second and Epic on the third. **Pathfinder's Chart** adds seeded mystery encounters with gold wagers, relic brokers, and optional elite bounties.

The starter relic draft rotates build options. **Towline Knot** rewards following displacement with a hit on the same enemy, **Bridgeplate** turns absorbed damage into a counter, and **Brass Metronome** rewards leaving a beat of space before a damaging tile. Their icons flash when they trigger. Battle rewards reserve a slot for an owned tile when possible. Hover or focus a reachable elite to preview its formation and a guaranteed reward choice.

Fresh expeditions can draft moves such as one-beat strikes, shots that create distance, and marks that any hero can spend on the next hit. The **Combat tiles** picker in Arena Practice includes the class expansion and all 24 Arsenal tiles.

Your first elite victory offers a relic draft before the tile rewards, which include each hero's signature Uncommon. Picking a relic can add a compatible tile to that reward. **Afterimage** lets an attack reach from the spot you blinked away from. **Barrage Rack** strips Guard on a quick follow-up against the same enemy. **Siege Fuse** pulls enemies together before a heavy attack. Battle Review records the tile and beat that triggered each effect.

Seeded battle and elite formations can include a Rivet Bombardier, Kiln Duelist, Relay Mender, or Dock Corsair. The encounter preview shows who you will face.

Medium starts solo. After the Tollkeeper, take a Legendary tile into your build and recruit a second hero with a starter tile. Both lanes fire on the same beat clock. Push or pull the Gilded Marshal to expose its armor, then land a hit with the other hero before it closes. Defeating the Marshal unlocks Brass Frontier Hard and Glass Foundry Easy. Each hero can take a Legendary trophy after the final boss.

Glass Foundry Easy is a solo route that ends at the Crucible Twins. Hunters prioritize the hero with the most ranged tile damage. Skirmishers prioritize the hero with the lowest current HP. The clear awards Aether Marks and records the unlock for Glass Foundry Medium.

Glass Foundry Medium starts solo. After the Crucible Twins, take a Legendary tile and recruit a second hero for the next leg. The Prism Warden pressures the hero with the most ranged damage and the hero with the lowest current HP. Push or pull to open its ward, then hit it with the other hero within three beats. A clear records the unlocks for Glass Foundry Hard and Crown Relay Easy.

Glass Foundry Hard adds the Tempering Causeway and a third hero after the Prism Warden. When the Tempered Colossus charges Forge Sweep, push or pull it with one hero, crack its guard with a hit from a second hero, and shatter it with a hit from the third. Shattering the guard cancels Forge Sweep for the rest of the fight.

Crown Relay Easy is a solo expedition through the skybridge outposts. Relay Guards shield nearby allies. Defeat the guard or pull one enemy away from the other to break the link. Time a Bash during the Relay Captain's windup to interrupt the charged strike. The first clear records the unlock for Crown Relay Medium.

Crown Relay Medium starts solo. Take a Legendary tile after defeating the Relay Captain and recruit a second hero for the High Relay Gate. While the Relay Marshal charges, separate the pair with one hero, then Bash the Marshal with your other hero before the link returns. A clear earns the unlocks for Crown Relay Hard and Moonwreck Docks Easy.

Crown Relay Hard adds the Crown Causeway and a third hero after the Relay Marshal. During Crown Charge, assign different heroes to break the Left and Right Guards' links. Bash the Crown Regent with your third hero while both links are down. Each link has its own reconnect timer. Defeating a guard removes its link from the fight.

Moonwreck Docks Easy is a solo expedition through a wrecked harbor. Winch Operators pull Dock Bruisers into striking range. Target the Operator or push the Bruiser away before it swings. The Wreckmaster pulls your hero in with a harpoon before slamming the area around it. Bash it after the pull to move the slam out of reach. A clear earns the Moonwreck Docks Medium unlock.

Moonwreck Docks Medium adds Breakwater Gate and a second hero after the Wreckmaster. The Tidekeeper harpoons both heroes and winds up Breakwater Slam. Push it with one hero to break the anchor, then push or pull it with the other hero before the slam lands. A clear earns the Moonwreck Docks Hard unlock.

Moonwreck Docks Hard adds Blackwater Gate and a third hero after the Tidekeeper. The Drowned Admiral reels the party in before charging Undertow Sweep. Break its anchor with a push, reverse the undertow with a pull from a second hero, then interrupt the sweep with a push from the third. Stagger the tiles across all three timelines so they finish in that order before the sweep lands.

Brass Frontier Hard has three legs. Recruit after the Tollkeeper and the Gilded Marshal. When the Iron Regent raises its seals, land damaging hits from three different heroes within four beats to break them.

## Arena practice

1. Start with **Two heroes**. The preset schedules Hexwright's **Pull · Farthest** at beat 4 and Warden's **Bash · Nearest** at beat 6. Start the battle and watch the tether bring the distant clockwork within striking distance.
2. **Reset fight**, change Pull's rule to **Nearest**, and compare the target and follow-up. Each tile previews its target in setup and chooses again when it fires.
3. Open **Tile storage** above the timeline. Drag tiles in either direction while the popup is open. Neighbors shift in the preview if they fit. You can also select a tile and click a clear starting beat or storage cell. Use the popup to discard a selected tile.
4. Try **Solo hero**, 2× speed, or a layout using Blink. Reset returns to setup and retains the current layout. Changing fixtures loads that fixture's preset.
5. Open **Tile storage** to find three touching Bash tiles. Use **Combine → Yes**, inspect any rarity, then confirm one of the two upgrades offered at the next rarity. Three four-beat Slams fit together on a full timeline. In an expedition, a camp can also advance a single Slam if the upgrade fits.
6. Choose **Forge practice** from the fixture picker to start with three Epic Sunwalls on Warden's lane. Combine them into either Legendary, then try the result in battle or move it into storage.
7. Choose **Blink forge practice** to inspect the full Blink family and try its teleport upgrades without spending Aether Marks.
8. Choose **Shot and Guard practice** to forge ranged and defensive tiles and try them in the arena.

The simulation runs at 60 ticks per second, with 21 ticks per beat and 12 beats per loop. Each tile fires at its right edge, including at beat 12 before the playhead wraps. Same-tick activations run in hero order. The arena is continuous and uses scripted movement and separation, capped displacement, and checked teleport destinations.

The practice palette supplies unlimited base tiles. Switching practice fixtures resets their inventory.

## Build

With matching Godot export templates installed, create a `build` folder and export **Windows Desktop**. The preset embeds the game data in the x86-64 executable.

Run the scripts under `tests/` with Godot's `--headless --script` options to check practice combat, setup controls, and the Frontier run.

## Desktop launcher

On Windows, `tools/Install-DesktopShortcut.ps1` installs the launcher in `%USERPROFILE%\Games\Brass Aether` and creates the **Brass & Aether** desktop shortcut. The launcher opens the cached game immediately and checks the project's GitHub `main` branch in the background. An update takes effect the next time you open the game. The updater uses Git, Godot 4.7.2, and the matching Windows release template.
